# imperfect.os

Product source for imperfect computers, **public and AGPL-3.0-only**. The public
name and domain are **imperfect computers** / **imperfect.computer** — the brand
is the domain, this repository is the machine. They were separated on
2026-09-17: the machine app is `locusrifle/imperfect.os`, and
`locusrifle/imperfect.computer` is the account door, which is private.

This tree is being dogfooded as the local laptop window. Do not put fleet keys,
account stores, or customer data here. Do not duplicate the door
(`locusrifle/imperfect.computer`, checked out at
`vaita:/home/dacre/imperfect-door`).
